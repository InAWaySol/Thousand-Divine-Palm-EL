



	1. a Trio of Buttons, Green Starts Looking or a trade, Yellow (Simulate) Is the backtesting Button, Magenta Saves all your Collected data to a TImestamped Folder

	2. Five Buttons, ITs for the Weighted Class system for the Hands, Implemented, Not yet utilized, The center button resets to Neutral.

	3. Blue Shows the # of hands for Buying are Active and Red is for Selling, The numbers Below Show the Total Active of the total had.

	4. Blue is for Buy, Red is for Selling, Shows the Apps Consensus and nearness to Sealing the Deal on a trade.

	5. Price chart displays price Relative not to 0 but relative to Itself, A division spread between the Lowest and the highest Price inputted to the chart within the currently displayed table

	6. An artistic Image of the Goddess Guanyin, Displays what Percentage of ALL hands For either Left(SELL) or Right(Buy) are currently active (rounds up If over)

	7. I call it the "Fog Map" A quick and efficient way to display the activity of over 1000 unique algorithms, Also plays a neat animation at the start 

	8. HSRC aka Hand Sync Rate Chart, Shows the Current Consensus of all the hands as it changes over time, Switches between Buying and Selling Hands Automatically

	9. Sliding bar, Shows the MOST RECENTLY activated Hands For the Current Objective, Buying or Selling

	10. The HandBook Home for Descriptions and Modulations of all Algorithms. 

	11. Buy Point and Sell Point SLiders, Allows you to decide what percent of the algorithms need to be active for you to Seal the Deal on a trade.
